C Route

arrow_back All Routes & Shuttles

The C shuttle operates continuously between the Starvine parking deck at Clairmont campus and the Woodruff Circle Transit Hub, Monday - Friday 5:00 a.m. - 12:00 a.m.

During weekends (Saturday through Sunday, 8:30 a.m. - 12:00 a.m.), the C route operates on a fixed schedule.
