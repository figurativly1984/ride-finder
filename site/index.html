Routes are planned over the published schedules of MARTA, Xpress, Ride Gwinnett and CobbLinc plus Emory's shuttle timetables, all together, so every combination of shuttles, buses and trains is considered. Live bus delays and train arrivals come from MARTA's real-time feeds when available. Shuttles don't run on university holidays.
